> **Rule #1:** Read this before anything else. 2 minutes. Every day.

---

## Step 1 — Open the Dashboard (30 sec)

Open Obsidian → the Dashboard is the homepage.
Look at your objectives and principles.

**Ask yourself:** Which of my principles am I most likely to break today?

---

## Step 2 — Check the Objective (30 sec)

Look at the "Current Objective" section in the Dashboard.
**Ask yourself:** What task do I need to do TODAY that moves me toward the objective?

Today isn't about "being productive." It's about **the one thing that matters.**

---

## Step 3 — Check the Traps (30 sec)

Look at your frequent traps in the Dashboard.
**Ask yourself:** Which trap did I fall into yesterday? Which one do I risk repeating today?

---

## Step 4 — Choose ONE Task (30 sec)

One single task for the next 2 hours.

**Don't:** "Working on project" (too vague)
**Do:** "Finish analysis X" (specific)

---

## ⚠️ If you get stuck

1. **Fell into overthinking?** → Take the smallest possible step.
2. **Fell into perfectionism?** → Launch messy. Fix later.
3. **Feeling lazy?** → Action > theory. Do something REAL.
4. **Feeling overwhelmed?** → One single task. Ignore the rest. Focus 2 hours.

---

## 🔄 At the end of the day

- What did you do today? (one sentence)
- What did you learn? (one sentence)
- What do you do tomorrow? (one sentence)

Write in the journal or in SESSION.md.

---

*Don't skip this. 2 minutes in the morning = the difference between chaos and execution.*
